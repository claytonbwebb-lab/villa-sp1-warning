📅 Day-by-Day Chronology

These issues were not discovered after the fact. They were reported to the property representative while we were still at the villa.

Day 1 — 23 Aug

Strong smell throughout villa from arrival. AC controllers showing "Time to clean filter" warning. Report made to property representative.

Day 2–3 — 24–25 Aug

Smells persist. Bedding changed twice by cleaners because smells returned. One bedroom must be kept closed and unused. Reports continued.

Day 4 — 26 Aug

CMS AC contractor attends — heavily contaminated filters and internal deposits photographed before cleaning. Technician confirms condition "not normal for a holiday rental."

Day 5 — 27 Aug

New repetitive AC noise develops — approximately every 8–10 seconds overnight, affecting two bedrooms. Second contractor visit required.

Days 5–6 — 27–28 Aug

Pool becomes cloudy again despite earlier maintenance. Property manager acknowledges from photographs. Pool maintenance re-arranged. Smells remain in bedrooms. AC noise ongoing.

Day 7 — 29 Aug

Worsening pool condition. Additional documentation and video recorded. Multiple outstanding defects still unresolved.

Day 8 — 30 Aug

Final walkthrough video recorded immediately before checkout. Formal claim submitted to Sealand Villas.

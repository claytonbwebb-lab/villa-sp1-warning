❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about this villa, answered based on documented evidence.

Is Villa SP1 Suites Santa Ponsa worth booking?
Based on documented evidence from an August 2026 stay, we would not recommend booking this property. Issues included air conditioning failure, severe persistent smells making one bedroom unusable, degraded pool water quality, unsafe trampoline, rusted outdoor furniture, and damaged umbrellas.
Does Villa SP1 Suites match its listing photos?
No. The listing photos show pristine outdoor furniture, undamaged umbrellas, clean artificial turf, a tidy garden shed, and well-maintained outdoor facilities. The reality included worn/dirty furniture, severely damaged umbrellas with frayed fabric, rusted BBQ, debris-covered artificial turf, a cluttered/weathered shed, and an unsafe trampoline.
What was wrong with the air conditioning?
AC controllers displayed 'Time to clean filter' maintenance warnings from arrival. A contractor found heavily contaminated filters and significant internal deposits. After cleaning, a new repetitive noise developed approximately every 8–10 seconds overnight, requiring a second technical visit.
Is the pool safe to use?
Pool water became cloudy and murky repeatedly throughout the stay despite professional maintenance. Underwater tile markings and dark deposits were visible. A prior guest on Airbnb (July 2026) also reported the pool cleaning system did not function well.
Can children safely use the garden facilities?
No. The garden had an unsafe shed considered hazardous around children, and a trampoline with destroyed padding, exposed foam, rusted frame, and debris on the jumping surface — meaning children were not allowed to use it.
How was the refund request resolved?
Guests paid €15,142 for a substantially diminished stay. They requested a 50% refund (€7,517). Sealand Villas offered €2,147 (approximately 13.6% of total) which was rejected as inadequate. The matter was escalated to Booking.com under reference 5630494883.
